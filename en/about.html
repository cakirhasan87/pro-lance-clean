About Us

Pro-Lance's story and mission

Pro-Lance was founded by two IT professionals, each with more than 15 years of experience—one specializing in SAP technologies, the other in broader enterprise IT. Prior to launching Pro-Lance, we had already been active in IT resourcing through Smartia Limited and individual consultancy.

Over time, we recognized a consistent gap in the market: organizations were struggling to find not just qualified consultants, but adaptable, business-minded professionals who could bring clarity, momentum, and measurable value to digital transformation efforts.

That's how Pro-Lance was born.

Pro-Lance was created to continue that foundation—bringing together high-quality consultants under a scalable, professional structure.

From day one, we focused on staying lean, trusted, and deeply involved. Rather than taking a high-volume, transactional approach, we've built Pro-Lance around strong relationships—with both clients and consultants.

As our team and client base grew, so did the scope of our services. We began supporting more industries and expanded our reach across markets worldwide.

Today, Pro-Lance continues to evolve. We're proud and excited to now include AI-relevant technologies in our scope, helping clients bridge their legacy systems with intelligent solutions that unlock new possibilities.

Our hands-on experience allows us to understand the real-world challenges of digital transformation and deliver flexible, trusted resourcing solutions across Europe.

What sets us apart is not just experience—but empathy, responsiveness, and integrity. We know that digital transformation requires more than software—it requires the right people, placed at the right time, with the right mindset.

Whether you're looking for a trusted partner to guide your next transformation, or a highly capable team to extend your in-house capacity, Pro-Lance is ready to support you—with clarity, speed, and care.
